Pilates

5 Reasons to Start
Pilates Today

📅 17 August 2025 · 5 min read · The Workout Warehouse

Pilates has a reputation problem. Most people picture leotards, ballet barres, and graceful women stretching in a sunlit studio. So it gets written off — especially by gym-goers who think it's too gentle, too slow, or just not for them.

They're wrong. And here's why Pilates might be the missing piece in your training.

01. It Builds Real Core Strength

Not just abs — your entire trunk. Pilates targets the deep stabilising muscles that most gym exercises skip entirely: your transverse abdominis, pelvic floor, and spinal erectors. These are the muscles that keep you upright, protect your lower back, and transfer power through every lift, sprint, and jump you do.

The result? A stronger foundation for everything else you train. Heavier squats. Better posture. Less back pain.

02. It Fixes the Imbalances You Don't Know You Have

Most of us have a dominant side. A tight hip. A shoulder that sits slightly forward. Years of the same movements compound these patterns until something eventually gives.

Pilates is built around controlled, precise movement — which means it exposes and corrects these imbalances before they become injuries. It's the kind of work that keeps you in the gym long-term, not sidelined.

03. It Genuinely Improves Flexibility and Mobility

Static stretching after a workout is fine. But Pilates moves your body through its full range of motion under control — which is how you actually build usable flexibility. The kind that carries over into sport, lifting, and everyday life.

If you've been stiff for years, a few weeks of Pilates will show you what you've been missing.

04. It's Low-Impact — But Never Easy

Pilates is kind to your joints. There's no pounding, no heavy loading, no impact. That makes it ideal for anyone recovering from injury, managing a chronic condition, or just looking for a training day that doesn't wreck them.

Don't mistake low-impact for low-intensity. A quality Pilates session will leave your core shaking and your mind clear. It's a different kind of hard — and that's exactly why it works.

05. It Trains the Mind-Muscle Connection

Pilates demands focus. You can't zone out — every movement is intentional. That level of body awareness carries directly into your other training, making you more efficient, more controlled, and less injury-prone across the board.

Athletes who add Pilates often describe it as the training that made everything else click. Suddenly your glutes fire when they're supposed to. Your shoulders stay packed. Your breathing is controlled.

Pilates isn't a replacement for your training — it's the upgrade your training has been waiting for.
